# Sharing Your Card and Badge

## README badge

Replace `YOUR_LOGIN`:

```md
[![CCWarriors](https://api.ccwarriors.xyz/badge/YOUR_LOGIN.svg)](https://ccwarriors.xyz/YOUR_LOGIN?ref=badge)
```

Rank, tier, and 30-day burn, refreshed about hourly. Or hit **Copy README badge** on your card at [ccwarriors.xyz](https://ccwarriors.xyz) — same snippet, no typing.

## Profile links

Your profile is `ccwarriors.xyz/<login>` — short and clean. Older `ccwarriors.xyz/u/<login>` links keep working forever.

## Social unfurls

When you paste your profile link into X, Discord, Slack, Telegram, or WhatsApp, the preview renders your rank — and your archetype, if you've unlocked insights and kept them public. Share buttons on the site use the `/u/` form of the link; that's the one wired for previews, so prefer it when pasting manually too.

## Story link

Once you've unlocked your story ([Deep Insights and Stories](../profiles-and-insights/deep-insights.md)), it lives at `ccwarriors.xyz/<login>/story` — a separate page made for sharing.
