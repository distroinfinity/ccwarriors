# Profile Pages

Your profile lives at `ccwarriors.xyz/<your-github-login>`.

## From sync data alone

No opt-ins needed for any of this:

- **Rhythm** — a daily-burn heatmap of the past year, plus your current and longest streaks.
- **Efficiency** — a grade from A+ to D based on your **cache hit ratio**: how much of your context gets reused from the prompt cache instead of re-sent. (It's deliberately not about which model you use — for power users, Opus *is* the tool.) Plus your model mix and tokens per active day.
- **Per-tool split** — where your burn actually goes.
- **GitHub stats** — your verified public footprint: account age, followers, stars, merged PRs, commit streaks.
- **Your card** — tier, rank, scene, and the README badge copy button.

## With deep insights unlocked

Opting in ([how and what it sends](deep-insights.md)) adds the behavioral layer: your working-style axes and archetype, habit stats, a deck of shareable insight cards (pin your favorite four to the front), and the **Craft Score** — six pillars (Direction, Verification, Autonomy, Yield, Orchestration, Throughput) folded into one score with forge-themed tiers: **Apprentice → Journeyman → Artisan → Mastersmith**. Scores are marked *provisional* until enough warriors have opted in for percentiles to mean something.

## You control visibility

On your own profile: unlock or disable insights, flip them public/private without deleting anything, see exactly what's stored, and purge your deep data entirely. Visitors can't tell the difference between "never opted in" and "turned it off" — locked is locked.
