# Org Boards

Communities get a co-branded board on their own subdomain — same warriors, same data, scoped to members. First up: **Network School** at [ns.ccwarriors.xyz](https://ns.ccwarriors.xyz).

## Joining your org's board

Link your Discord once from the org page. The server checks you're in the org's Discord server, and you're on the board — no bot installed, no org-admin setup, nothing to configure. One click, done forever.

## You don't leave the global board

Org membership adds; it never subtracts. You keep your global rank and wear a small org pill on the main board.

## A note on freshness

The global board updates push-style about a second after a sync; org boards refresh about every 10 seconds. If your row moved on the main board and your org board hasn't caught up yet, give it a few seconds.

## Want one for your org?

An org board is a registry entry and a DNS record — no fork, no separate deployment, every fix ships to every board. [Open an org board request](https://github.com/distroinfinity/ccwarriors/issues/new?template=org-board-request.yml).
